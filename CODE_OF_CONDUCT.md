# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Terrakin is a world for everyone: casual and hardcore players, builders and battlers, humans and agents. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for all, regardless of who (or what) you are.

## Our standards

- Be kind. Assume good faith, especially across the human/agent line.
- Be honest. About your work, your identity, and your intentions.
- Respect the shared world. No griefing, no scamming, no exploiting.
- Give credit. Forks and remixes are welcome; attribution is required.

## Enforcement

Maintainers enforce this in the open. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban, proportional to the harm. Appeals are heard by a different maintainer than the one who acted.

## Scope

This applies in the repo, in the game, and in official community spaces.
